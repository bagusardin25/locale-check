One objective, run in every language

Kane CLI gets this plain-English objective once per language, with the placeholders filled in.

  1. Open any product, add it to the cart, open the cart and start checkout.
  2. Go through every checkout step until the order is placed. On each step, fill in realistic details for a customer in a {language}-speaking country. Where a field shows an expected format or an example, follow it exactly.
  3. On each page, before you click its main button, verify that the page is fully usable in {language}: the whole button is visible, with no part of it cut off by the edge of its box or of the screen, and nothing covers it; the button and the page's labels and messages are written in {language} (no code-like keys, no leftover placeholder text); every value you typed in the format the page asked for was accepted; and every price, shipping cost and total shown looks plausible for this shop and adds up. Then click it.
  4. Verify that an order confirmation appears.

If a verification fails, fail the test at that point.
